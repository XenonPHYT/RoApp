About RoApp

A smarter way to manage community applications.

What RoApp is

RoApp is an application platform for Roblox communities that run on Discord. Staff design forms in the dashboard, applicants apply inside Discord, and reviewers work through applications privately, with a clear history of every decision.

How it is built

  • Per-community: each community has its own forms, applications, staff access and settings.
  • Private by design: internal notes never reach applicants, and access is checked against Discord on every request.
  • Transparent: status changes and staff actions are recorded in an audit history.

Independence

RoApp is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Discord or Roblox. Discord and Roblox are trademarks of their respective owners.
