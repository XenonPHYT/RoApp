Sign-in problems

“Sign-in could not be verified”

This means the sign-in started in one browser and finished in another, the page was opened twice, or cookies are blocked. Close extra tabs, allow cookies for this site, and start again from Sign in with Discord in a single browser. On phones, avoid starting from a link inside another app’s built-in browser.

Signed out unexpectedly

Sessions end after a period of inactivity, when you sign out, or when Discord stops accepting your sign-in. Select Sign in with Discord again.

My community does not appear

  • RoApp must be added to your Discord server.
  • The server owner must register the community once, with /setup or Set up a community in the dashboard. Installing the bot alone does not register it.
  • You must be a member of the server with the Discord account you signed in with.

I can see the community but not the dashboard

Dashboard access is given by the server owner to specific roles. Discord Administrator permission does not grant it. Ask the owner to add one of your roles in Staff & permissions.

Application problems

  • I cannot start or submit: the form may be closed, you may already have an active application, or you may be inside the form’s cooldown. The message shows which applies.
  • My answers disappeared: unfinished answers are kept for 24 hours. After that, or if the form was updated, you start again.
  • I did not get a message about my application: private messages depend on your Discord privacy settings. My applications always shows the current status.

Slash commands do not appear

Commands are registered by whoever operates this RoApp deployment. If none appear in your server, let the operator know at the email address below. Reopening Discord can also help after a change.

Still need help?

Ask your community’s staff first. For problems with RoApp itself, email official.roapp@gmail.com. When you report a problem, include what you were doing and the message you saw; never share passwords, tokens or secrets. Setup instructions are in the documentation.
