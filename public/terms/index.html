5. The Service and permitted use

5.1

  • create application forms in the dashboard, with several question types, and publish them as versioned forms;
  • publish panels in Discord where members choose a form and apply, in short steps, with a review screen before submitting;
  • review applications in a private workspace: a queue, reviewer assignments, internal notes, decisions with a reason and revision requests;
  • notify Applicants of status changes by private message, where the Community’s settings allow;
  • control which Discord roles can use the dashboard and which permissions each role has; and
  • see an audit history of staff actions.
