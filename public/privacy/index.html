Privacy Policy

What RoApp stores, why, and who can see it.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

What RoApp stores

  • Sign-in: your Discord user ID and username. A sign-in token from Discord is kept encrypted for the length of your session and is used to list your servers. Session identifiers are stored only as one-way hashes.
  • Communities: the server’s ID, name and owner, plus the forms, panels, staff roles and settings its managers configure.
  • Applications: your Discord user ID, the answers you submit (including any Roblox username you enter), the form version used, status changes and the public reason for a decision.
  • Staff records: assignments, internal notes and an audit history of staff actions. Internal notes are never shown to applicants.
  • Drafts: answers to an unfinished application are kept for 24 hours so you can resume, then removed.
  • Operational data: short-lived records used to secure sign-in and prevent repeated or abusive requests, and queued private messages about application updates.

Cookies

RoApp sets only the cookies needed to keep you signed in and to complete sign-in safely: a session cookie, a temporary sign-in cookie, and a small marker that lets returning users skip Discord’s approval screen. There are no advertising or analytics cookies. Signing out removes the session cookie and the marker.

Who can see what

  • You can see your own applications and their public status.
  • Staff of a community with the right permissions can see that community’s applications, answers and internal notes. They cannot see other communities’ data.
  • Roblox usernames you enter are sent to Roblox’s public lookup service to check that the account exists.
  • Discord receives the requests RoApp makes on your behalf, such as listing servers and sending private messages.

Retention and your choices

Applications, notes and audit history are kept until they are removed by the operator of this deployment; no automatic deletion of them is currently enabled. You can withdraw an application, sign out at any time, and revoke RoApp’s access in your Discord account settings. To ask about access to, correction of or removal of your data, contact the community that received your application or the operator of this RoApp deployment.

Security

Access is restricted per community and verified on the server. Data is stored in Cloudflare’s infrastructure. No system is perfectly secure, so avoid including sensitive personal information in application answers.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date above shows the latest revision.
